These context-appropriate word and phrase chunks are educational tokens. Each table is a small illustrative candidate list, rather than a real model’s full vocabulary. One word is not always one token. The probabilities are chosen to show the mechanism; they are not model or tokenizer outputs.

This example chooses the highest probability. Generation can also sample from the distribution. Either rule appends one token, then predicts from the new context.
